Each board is named by the last four characters of its MAC address and keeps its colour on every graph. "This board" is the one serving this page; signal is the last relay link for readings from other boards. History builds up while this page is open (up to 10 minutes); the board itself keeps only its latest 256 records. Gaps mean no reading arrived; no values are filled in. SGP30 and BH1750 placeholders are not graphed.
