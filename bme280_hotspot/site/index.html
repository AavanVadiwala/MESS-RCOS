Each sensor is named by the last four characters of its board ID and keeps its colour on every graph. When the brain is online it uploads every 10 seconds and this page shows those readings from anywhere. Connect brain needs Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop; live readings show only on the computer the brain is plugged into. Otherwise this page shows readings published about every 6 minutes by a computer running the bridge; GitHub Pages can add up to 10 minutes of caching, so expect readings to be 5–20 minutes old. Gaps mean a sensor or the brain was offline; no values are filled in.
